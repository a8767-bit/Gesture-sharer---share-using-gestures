
# 📱 Gesture Sharer

> Share links, photos, and screenshots between nearby Android devices using just hand gestures. **No menus to open, no files to choose, and no intrusive notifications.**

---

## ✨ Highlights

- 🤲 **100% Gesture-Controlled:** Just make a fist (**Grab**) on the sending phone and show an open palm (**Drop**) on the receiving phone.
- 🚀 **Speed & Convenience:** Forget the Android sharing menu. Transfer in seconds.
- 🔒 **Absolute Privacy:** The app runs 100% on your device. Your photos and links never pass through external servers.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy and Security First

- 🚫 **No internet:** The app explicitly blocks the network permission (`INTERNET`). No data leaves your device.
- 👀 **Zero recording:** Front camera frames are analyzed instantly in memory by **MediaPipe** and discarded immediately afterwards.
- 📡 **Direct Local Connection:** Uses **Google Nearby Connections** (Bluetooth/local Wi-Fi) only within a secure **5-second** window activated exclusively by you.

---

## 🚀 How to Use in 3 Steps

1. **Activate:** Open the app and turn on **Gesture Mode** (or use the quick tile in the notification bar).
2. **Grab (Sender):** On the sending phone, make a **closed fist** gesture in front of the front camera.
3. **Drop (Receiver):** On the receiving phone, show an **open palm** to the camera within 5 seconds.

Done! The content is received instantly, with a gentle vibration and no visual clutter.

---

## 📋 Quick Installation Guide

Since the app focuses on privacy and is installed via APK, Android requires a quick initial security clearance for the Accessibility Service:

1. Install the APK and open **Gesto Share**.
2. Grant the requested permissions (Camera, Nearby Devices, and Battery Optimization).
3. To enable **Accessibility** (required to capture the current link or screen):
   - If Android shows _"Restricted setting"_, go to **App Info** (_Settings > Apps > Gesto Share_).
   - Tap the **3 dots (`⋮`)** in the top right corner and select **Allow restricted settings**.
   - Go back to _Settings > Accessibility_ and enable **Gesto Share**.

---

## ⚙️ Requirements

- **Android 14+** (API 34 or higher).
- Updated **Google Play Services**.
- **Bluetooth and Wi-Fi** turned on (no need to be connected to an internet-enabled Wi-Fi network).
